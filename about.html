About Me

Hi, I'm Briggs Newton, a 25-year-old musician, teacher, and music producer based in Ogden, Utah. My musical journey began at the age of 10 when I taught myself how to play the piano. At 17, I picked up the guitar and became passionate about acoustic fingerpicking. By 22, I dove into the world of music production, and I’ve been creating ever since.

Music and teaching are my two greatest passions. I love helping people of all ages develop their skills and discover their sound. I’ve lived in Utah my whole life, with the exception of two years I spent living in Africa, serving others and immersing myself in new cultures. During that time, I learned to speak Portuguese and Spanish, experiences that broadened my perspective and deepened my love for connection through art.

My musical influences range from indie and alternative to pop and singer-songwriter. My heart is in the acoustic guitar, and fingerstyle playing is my specialty. I bring a unique and creative approach to teaching and producing music, with a goal to help every student find their voice and turn their playing into something truly lasting.
